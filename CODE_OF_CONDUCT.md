# Code of conduct

Thespis follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in its issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else it's discussed.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, criticise work rather than people, and accept that maintainers may
remove comments, commits or contributors that don't follow it.

## Reporting

Write to **CONTACT_TO_DECIDE** about anything that breaks it, including by a maintainer. Reports are kept
confidential, and the maintainers answer within a week. They act as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe:
from a private warning up to a ban from the project.
